Info.plist additions (iOS)
==========================

Merge these keys into ios/WayToGoRN/Info.plist so CoreLocation may range iBeacons
(Section 6). iOS requires a location usage description; iBeacon ranging uses
When-In-Use authorization.

  <key>NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription</key>
  <string>WayToGo uses your location to detect nearby Bluetooth beacons and show your position on the floor plan. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.</string>

  <key>NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription</key>
  <string>WayToGo uses Bluetooth to detect nearby beacons for indoor positioning.</string>

  <key>NSBluetoothPeripheralUsageDescription</key>
  <string>WayToGo uses Bluetooth to detect nearby beacons for indoor positioning.</string>

Notes
-----
- iBeacon ranging on iOS MUST use CoreLocation (CLBeacon), not CoreBluetooth;
  Apple strips iBeacon manufacturer bytes from CoreBluetooth scans.
- The WayToGoBeacon.swift module requests When-In-Use authorization on startScan.
- Add the Swift file to the target; Xcode will prompt to create a bridging header
  (keep the auto-generated WayToGoRN-Bridging-Header.h). The RCTEventEmitter/React
  imports resolve via the umbrella header once the Pods are installed.
